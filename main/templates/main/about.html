📖 Our Journey

Founded with Love in 2017

Sunshine Tots was born from a simple dream — to create a daycare where every child in Bamburi, Mombasa could feel as loved and cherished as they do at home. Our founder, Mama Amina Kamau, a certified Early Childhood Development educator with over 15 years of experience, opened our doors in January 2017 with just 12 children and a heart full of passion.

Today, we serve over 250 families across Mombasa County, and our team of 15+ dedicated caregivers continues to grow. Every sunrise brings new adventures, new discoveries, and new smiles in our colorful world of learning. We are proudly licensed by the Kenya Ministry of Education and certified by the National Council for Persons with Disabilities as an inclusive daycare.
