🌈 What We Offer

Our Amazing Programs & Services

Every program is designed to nurture your child's growth — body, mind, and heart.

🍼

Infant Care

Gentle, responsive care for babies aged 0–18 months. A home-like environment with trained caregivers who love what they do.

🧸

Toddler Program

Exciting discovery-based learning for children aged 18m–3 years. Language, motor skills, and social development through play.

📚

Pre-School Prep

Structured early learning for 3–6 year olds. Literacy, numeracy, science exploration, and Kenya curriculum-aligned activities.

🎨

Art & Creativity

Finger painting, crafts, and creative expression sessions that build confidence and unleash each child's unique imagination.

🎵

Music & Movement

Weekly music, singing, and dance classes that boost emotional intelligence, coordination, and joy in every little heart.

🥗

Nutritious Meals

Three balanced meals and two healthy snacks daily, prepared by our in-house nutritionist using fresh, local ingredients.
